## Project Summary

Wanjiku operates a fresh-produce stall at Gikomba Market and needs to understand her business performance using two weeks of daily sales records and current stock information. This analysis uses core Python to calculate sales totals, averages, weekly growth, daily performance categories, weekday and weekend comparisons, stock reorder requirements and gross profit margins. The findings are used to identify business strengths, stock management priorities and opportunities to improve sales.

## How to Run

Open the notebook in Google Colab: [Open in Colab](https://colab.research.google.com/github/topstar_cog/plp-da-a1-gikomba-stall-analysis/blob/main/stall_analysis.ipynb)

## Top Three Findings

1. Total sales over 14 days were **KES 46,450**, with Week 2 sales increasing by **10.66%** compared with Week 1.
2. Average weekend sales were **KES 4,900**, which was **82.50% higher** than the weekday average of KES 2,685.
3. **Tomatoes and Sukuma** require restocking because their stock quantities are below their reorder levels.
